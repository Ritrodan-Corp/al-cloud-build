Oracle A1 entitlement clarification research: Oracle's July 21, 2026 Customer Connect announcement, edited August 24, states the reduced Ampere A1 allocation applies to Always Free tenancies and is now 2 OCPUs / 12 GB total, equivalent to 1,500 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours monthly. Oracle's current Cloud Price List separately states each paid tenancy gets the first 3,000 A1 OCPU-hours and 18,000 GB-hours per month free, and Oracle's Arm Compute reference says paid and trial accounts receive the same 3,000 / 18,000 paid-tenancy allowance. Therefore the user's console notice that the Always Free entitlement change does not affect paid resources, existing paid workloads, or bill is consistent with Oracle's current paid-tenancy pricing. For the planned PAYG 4 OCPU / 16 GB fixed A1 VM, 31-day maximum usage is 2,976 OCPU-hours and 11,904 GB-hours, within the current paid-tenancy monthly free quantities.
