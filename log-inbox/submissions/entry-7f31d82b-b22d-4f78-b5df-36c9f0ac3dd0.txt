Scheduling policy refinement for fixed AL Cloud chat agents: treat refresh/reset times as opening availability windows, not mandatory execution times. Delay non-urgent chores such as shop checks until the latest already-scheduled invocation before their next refresh/deadline so tasks can be coalesced and the number of chat-agent runs minimized. Example for General Shop windows: inventory created at 00:00 can be checked at 08:00 before the 12:00 refresh; 12:00 inventory can be checked at 16:00 before 18:00; 18:00 inventory can be checked at 21:00 before 00:00. Apply the same deadline/window reasoning to other non-expiring chores where mechanics permit, rather than scheduling at reset boundaries by default.
