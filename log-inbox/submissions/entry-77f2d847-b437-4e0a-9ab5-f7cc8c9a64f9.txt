Cloudflare Pages clarification: Pages is suitable for hosting the static/PWA shell but does not solve connectivity to the VM-local controller/video backend. For this project, serving the existing UI from the controller origin through a protected route is simpler and preserves same-origin behavior. A split Pages frontend plus separate backend hostname would add CORS/Access/session complexity without clear benefit. Keep Pages optional for a future polished PWA shell, not as the primary connectivity mechanism.
