Cloudflare cost review for browser controller: Cloudflare Zero Trust/Access Free is $0 forever for up to 50 users. Domain registration remains a separate annual cost if a custom domain is needed; Cloudflare Registrar currently advertises at-cost pricing starting around $7.85 depending on TLD. Important caveat from current Cloudflare Tunnel docs: public-hostname routes on Free/Pro/Business are subject to service-specific restrictions for serving video/large files and point users to paid video products such as Cloudflare Stream. This makes a direct public-hostname design for the raw H.264 game stream potentially non-free/non-ideal. Private-network routing via Cloudflare One/WARP avoids that public-video restriction and can still fit the free Zero Trust tier for a single user, but requires the Cloudflare client. Conclusion: authentication/tunnel control plane can be free, domain may cost annually, but the exact public-browser streaming design may incur paid-media requirements; keep current IAP launcher as zero-cost baseline and reassess Cloudflare transport architecture before implementation.
