Oracle PAYG pricing review. Current May 2026 Oracle public price list for paid tenancies states Ampere A1 includes first 3,000 OCPU-hours and 18,000 GB-hours of memory each month at no charge; usage above that is $0.01/OCPU-hour and $0.0015/GB-hour. This means a 4 OCPU / 24 GB A1 VM can fit within the paid-tenancy monthly free A1 allowance even in a 31-day month (2,976 OCPU-hours and 17,856 GB-hours), subject to tenancy/region rules. Standard-flex compute billing pauses when OCI instance status is Stopped; persistent boot/block volumes remain separately billable if beyond applicable free storage allowance. OCI currently advertises 10 TB/month outbound data transfer free. Oracle documentation is inconsistent on older Free Tier A1 compute allowance (some Always Free docs still show 1,500 OCPU-hours / 9,000 GB-hours), so use the paid-tenancy global price list for PAYG planning and verify actual tenancy cost/usage limits before relying on free thresholds. Block-volume free-storage documentation also has conflicting 100/200 GB figures across Oracle pages, so verify the tenancy-specific allowance before provisioning storage above 100 GB.
