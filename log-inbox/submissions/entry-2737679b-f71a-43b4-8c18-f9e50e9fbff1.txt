Windows launcher wrapper step: added `windows-launcher/Azur Lane Cloud.vbs` as the user-facing one-click entry point. It invokes `azl-cloud-launch.cmd` invisibly, so successful launches do not leave a terminal window. This preserves the loopback-only VM service and existing IAP/SSH security model.
