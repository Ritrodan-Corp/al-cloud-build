Oracle free-tier CPU scaling constraint review: verified current Oracle 2026 documentation before recommending any A1 resize. Current docs distinguish account type. Always Free tenancies are documented at 1,500 A1 OCPU-hours and 9,000 GB-hours per month, equivalent to 2 OCPUs and 12 GB continuously. Paid/trial tenancies are documented as receiving the first 3,000 A1 OCPU-hours and 18,000 GB-hours per month free, roughly equivalent to 4 OCPUs and 24 GB continuously. Therefore an 8-OCPU resize cannot be assumed cost-free; on a paid tenancy it would exhaust a 3,000 OCPU-hour monthly allowance in about 375 hours if no other A1 use occurred, and on an Always Free tenancy it is outside the documented continuous free allocation. Next: establish the tenancy billing model and actual current-month A1 usage before any resize experiment.
