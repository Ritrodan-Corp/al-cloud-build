AL Cloud architecture refinement: User reports GPT-5.6 Luna is not capable enough to both decide gameplay strategy and perform computer-use interaction reliably. Revised model: keep Luna out of open-ended decision loops. Use a strong Director/planner to translate high-level account goals and current game state into narrow, explicit specialist jobs with bounded choices, stop conditions, and expected outputs. Cheap workers may still be viable for routine execution if prompts are highly constrained and authored by the stronger Director. Consider role/model tiering by difficulty: simple Tactical or deterministic maintenance may be eligible for Luna after testing; commissions may require a strong planner or carefully compiled policy; grinding likely needs a stronger model because of long-horizon UI recovery, fleet/stage/morale/resource management. To minimize Work usage, normal Chat can remain the interactive Director that compiles durable goal/policy state, while unattended wake events route to specialized workers; ambiguous/conflicting/threshold events can escalate to a stronger Work run. No VM or Android state modified.
