Cold launch returned Status=ok/MainActivity and the process remained stable instead of entering the ~2-second abort/PID-churn behavior seen on the 48-bit stock kernel. Post-launch scans showed Unity 2022.3.62f3 initializing with no Azur Lane SIGABRT, destroyed-mutex FORTIFY, Pastel/LLVM crash, or fatal signal 6 signature.
