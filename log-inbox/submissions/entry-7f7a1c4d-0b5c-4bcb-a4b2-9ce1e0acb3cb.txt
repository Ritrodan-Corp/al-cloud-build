Oracle A1 PAYG entitlement evidence review: current Oracle price-list and Arm Compute pages still publish 3,000 A1 OCPU-hours and 18,000 GB-hours monthly for paid/PAYG tenancies, while Always Free-only tenancies are now documented at 1,500 OCPU-hours / 9,000 GB-hours. Community validation is supportive but not absolute: a PAYG 4-OCPU/24-GB user reported a full July billing month at zero compute cost, including remaining at zero beyond the 375-hour point where the reduced allowance would have exhausted; Reddit reports also show multi-week PAYG 4/24 use at $0. Counterexamples found were either ambiguous billing cases or attributable to additional hidden A1 instances, and post-August-18 failures mainly involve already-disabled Always Free instances upgraded during/after enforcement rather than clean new PAYG provisioning. As of 2026-09-15 I did not find a clean newly-created post-August-18 PAYG 4/24 instance that has completed a full billing month and publicly documented $0. Treat the 3,000/18,000 PAYG entitlement as strongly supported, not yet empirically certain for this tenancy. After PAYG activation, verify plan type and live cost/usage, create the bounded 4-OCPU/16-GB candidate only after entitlement checks, and monitor Cost Analysis/budget alerts during validation.
