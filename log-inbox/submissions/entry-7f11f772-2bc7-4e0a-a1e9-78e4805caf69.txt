Centralized raw-log intake is now validated end to end after enabling the Google Docs API. A production log-only submission on vm-control triggered the authorized gcp-vm-chat-control workflow, classified log_intake=true, skipped the VM lifecycle job, authenticated the existing alcloud-vm-lifecycle service account through WIF with Docs-only scope, and appended the report to the AL Cloud Raw Log as R000204 with stable Submission-ID github-file:entry-4d74a772-cb5c-4dce-9b85-797f4b6c842d. A deliberate retry of the same log_drain job processed the unchanged inbox successfully and returned R000204 again rather than allocating R000205, proving idempotent duplicate suppression. The experiment agent therefore does not need VM-local Google credentials or manual R-number coordination; it only needs to create a new immutable log-inbox/submissions/entry-<uuid>.txt report on vm-control. Validation artifacts outside the entry-*.txt production namespace remain ignored by the drainer.
