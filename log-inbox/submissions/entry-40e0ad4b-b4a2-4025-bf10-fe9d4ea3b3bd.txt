Oracle PAYG/free-credit clarification: Official OCI Free Tier documentation still states a $300 promotional credit valid for up to 30 days. This can be used to calibrate paid-resource usage/overage during the trial window, but it is not a permanent monthly cushion and may already be expired on an existing tenancy. For AL Cloud planning, treat Always Free/PAYG monthly allowances as the durable baseline and the $300 trial credit only as a temporary calibration buffer if the tenancy still has it available.
