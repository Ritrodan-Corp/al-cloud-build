Performance research constrained to the production ALAS target. Treat the Oracle tenancy as PAYG for this project and do not use Always Free tenancy limits in planning. ALAS upstream code hard-rejects non-1280x720 screenshots and its README recommends Azur Lane Frame Rate Settings = 60 FPS. Current ReDroid remains 1280x720 at 30 Hz and Azur Lane presents mostly at 66.7 ms intervals (~15-16 FPS), with occasional 33.3 ms frames, consistent with repeatedly missing a 30 Hz vsync deadline rather than an arbitrary low-rate cap. ReDroid guest mode selects ANGLE when available, layered over SwiftShader Vulkan; upstream SwiftShader has deprecated its direct GLES frontend and recommends ANGLE-over-SwiftShader, while ReDroid users report instability forcing the old direct SwiftShader path. Oracle exposes no guest cpufreq/governor controls in this VM.
