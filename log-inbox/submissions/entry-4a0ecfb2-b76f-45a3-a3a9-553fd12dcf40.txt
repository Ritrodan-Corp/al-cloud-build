Fixed-time daily-task simplification. Proposed separating deterministic reset-time chores from the more complex Director/timer system. A narrow scheduled Daily Reset agent can handle tasks whose availability occurs on a known clock, such as login rewards, skill-training participation, exercises, daily raids, hard-mode attempts, dorm refill, and reset-time shop checks, while variable-duration completions and adaptive farming remain under Director-style orchestration. The fixed-time agent should still read durable user goals/policies for choices such as shop purchases and training selection, use idempotent completion markers/checklists so retries are safe, and fail closed on unexpected/destructive states. Prefer one bounded reset routine or a small number of fixed-schedule routines over many permanent specialists; split only if runtime/reliability makes a single midnight batch unwieldy.
