Researched fixed-time Azur Lane daily mechanics before drafting scheduled-agent policy. Verified current EN timing and task mechanics from current official notices, wiki/community references, and current AzurLaneAutoScript behavior. Findings: EN current notices use UTC-7; Daily Raid has 3 daily attempts for the rotating challenge, Tactical Training has its own daily attempts, and Supply Line Disruption is limited to twice weekly; Hard Mode has 3 daily entries and failure/retreat does not consume an entry; Exercises refill 5 attempts at 00:00, 12:00, and 18:00 server time, with a cap of 10 stored attempts, so a midnight-only routine cannot cover the full daily exercise allowance; dorm has six food types with different supply/EXP effects and oil-vs-gem implications, so dorm refill policy is not deterministic; Tactical Class requires explicit ship/skill/book policy and is inherently duration-based rather than only daily-reset-based; General Shop refreshes every 6 hours, Merit daily at 00:00, Guild Monday/Friday at 00:00, and Core/Medal/Prototype-class shops have monthly/reset-specific behavior. Remaining questions are user policy choices (dorm food/spending, skill priorities/book policy, hard-mode stage objective, shop buy list, exercise scheduling), not unknown game mechanics.
