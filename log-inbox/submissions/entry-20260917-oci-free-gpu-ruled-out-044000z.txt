2026-09-17: Researched whether the current PAYG OCI tenancy has any ongoing free GPU-backed Compute VM option. Oracle's current Always Free Compute list includes VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro and VM.Standard.A1.Flex only; GPU shapes are not included. Oracle currently lists VM.GPU.A10.1 as a paid shape at about $2.62/hour. New-account Free Trial credits can be spent on eligible services for up to 30 days, but that is temporary promotional credit rather than an ongoing free GPU tier and does not apply as a persistent free GPU option for this established PAYG tenancy. Conclusion: rule out migration to a free OCI GPU VM. Any OCI GPU migration would incur cost and, for A10 VM shapes, move the workload to Intel/x86 rather than the current Ampere ARM platform.
