Centralized raw-log idempotency acknowledgement bug fixed during canonical reconciliation. A replay exposed that tools/al-log-append.py located an existing submission by taking the last RNNNNNN token before its Submission-ID marker. If an entry title mentioned another R-number, for example `reconciled through R000209`, the duplicate was correctly not appended but the helper could acknowledge the wrong historical R-number. The same broad token scan also made next-ID calculation depend on R-number mentions in prose.

Production vm-control commit f33b42c20912f6fb73f3bbd766843016fa75344d and main commit b1b2d248f4721d16b2c6cd989e0a433d758f1bb6 replace broad R-token matching with multiline entry-header matching only: actual `RNNNNNN | ...` header lines determine both next-ID allocation and the owning entry for an existing Submission-ID. The internal helper content is identical on both branches after the fix. No raw chronology entry was rewritten or duplicated; the defect affected retry acknowledgement/lookup semantics, not the already-materialized entries.
