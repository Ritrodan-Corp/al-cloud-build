Reviewed the authoritative Azur Lane Cloud Running Progress Log to reconcile display-resolution history before further ALAS integration decisions. The current-state document records the qualified baseline as a default 1280x720 physical display, but Experiment 157 separately established a lower-resolution performance configuration: actual display-size reduction to 854x480 at 12 Hz materially improved the two-core software-rendered floor, averaging about 156% container CPU without encoding and about 161% with bounded H.264 around 2 Mbps while maintaining 12 FPS. Technical Reference also records an integrated browser-streaming measurement at 854x480/12 Hz of about 148% container CPU versus about 112% baseline. Therefore 1280x720 should not be treated as the only relevant geometry for ALAS reuse; low-resolution 854x480 is a previously tested and important target configuration, while the current qualified baseline service remains documented as default 1280x720 physical display. Future ALAS template/coordinate reuse should account for both facts and avoid assuming direct 1280x720 drop-in compatibility without checking the active display configuration.
